# DONUT-PROG-01: Donut's Floor 01 ability progression, reconciled spec

**Status:** design adopted by recommendation under the owner's design-defaults directive (2026-11-21), Revision 1 (section 13). DP-A, DP-B and DP-C are implemented (sections 14 and 15); the rest is not. Every value is a data knob with a question ID (DP-Q01..DP-Q47), not approved balance. Game feel needs the owner's playtest. Delivery is split into small PRs in the [work order](../07-agents/donut-prog-01-workorder.md).

**Designer tier:** top tier (reconciles a detailed owner request with the controller contract, three in-flight designs and existing code; checks the story chronology). Execution: medium tier. Review: an independent top-tier agent per PR.

**Owner request (2026-11-21), summarized:** Donut starts Floor 01 with agile movement, higher and farther jumps, the defensive swat and the pounce (0–20 %). The Tutorial Guild biscuit permanently unlocks Magic Missile and mana (~20 %), keeping the 8-charge pool, its regeneration and the primary mapping. "Light on Your Feet" permanently improves jump reach by about 20 % (~30 %), with no double jump and no new control. Then 30–70 % teaches ranged combat, mana, elevated firing, pounce finishers, environment and coordinated attacks through encounters, not abilities; 70–85 % asks for mastery against mixed enemies. After the Juicer (~85 %) Donut permanently gets Puddle Jumper: Circle/B, a short directional teleport, 112 px, 2 charges, 9 s cooldown, a valid, visible, collision-safe destination, no walls or protected gates crossed, no invulnerability, no damage, data-driven, no new button or equipment UI. The Ball of Swine tests the whole kit with no new ability. Unlocks are narrative, permanent, deterministic, persistent and checkpoint-safe; percentages are pacing targets only. The 12 s reflex evasion is DONUT-MOBILITY-01's and is not changed here. Final target: a fragile agile cat grows into a highly mobile magical fighter through positioning, ranged pressure, survival and tactical mobility, not damage scaling.

DONUT-PROG adds **rows, not a mechanism**. Everything below is CP-A's design (CP section 3, CP-Q44) applied to Donut.

| `special_short_blink` | 1 | `special` (the Circle/B special) | `[jump_reach_boost]` | CP's Juicer trigger `brute_boss_f01_defeated`, second entry of `grants: [fist_reinforced, special_short_blink]` (DP-Q03) |

Display names (`LIGHT FEET`, `PUDDLE JUMPER`) live in the rows' `display` field and the fan layer only.

- **Data:** the two rows, the one zone trigger, the appended grant and Donut's pin rows go into CP-A's `assets/data/gameplay/story_unlocks_v0.json`; DP-A re-pins that file's FNV hash (a data pin, not a golden). No schema change. Light on Your Feet and Puddle Jumper knobs live in a new `assets/data/gameplay/donut_prog_v0.json` (knob names start with their question ID, `dpq14_...`), loaded, validated and FNV-pinned by a new `src/donut_prog.rs`, as `carl_prog_v0.json` is.
- **Lifetimes (CP-Q07):** a grant survives a defeat retry, a checkpoint respawn, a solo swap and a 2P join or drop; a fixture restart restores the declared pin. There is no cross-session save; "persistent" means run-persistent, and the ledger's sorted, versioned snapshot carries Donut's ids for the future campaign save.
- **Hash:** nothing while empty (CP-A); Donut's rows add nothing to an unflagged run.
- **Event:** `CombatEvent::CapabilityGranted { tick, hero, capability }` with `hero` = Donut (`DONUT_HERO` 1).

**Per-program enablement (requirement on CP-A; Revision 1, B1).** CP section 3 makes a scene's pin the union of the pin rows of the enabled story flags. Triggers need the same per-program rule, because the Juicer trigger is shared:

1. Each story flag enables one hero's **program**: `--carl-prog` enables hero 0 (Carl), `--donut-prog` enables hero 1 (Donut). A capability's program is its row's `hero`; no schema field is added. A scripted fixture declares its enabled programs next to its pin. By default they are the programs of the flags that own its pin rows, so CP's `carl_prog_*` fixtures enable Carl only.
2. **Pins:** a pin row is owned by its flag (CP). `--scene boss_f01` keeps **one pin row per hero**: CP's `f01_dynamite_held` (`--carl-prog`) and Donut's `f01_donut_boss_held` (`--donut-prog`, holds `jump_reach_boost`; `primary_ranged` is read through from the boss scene's existing unlocked biscuit). With both flags the scene's pin is their union.
3. **Triggers:** a trigger is evaluated only while at least one entry of its `grants` list belongs to an enabled program. When it fires, it is consumed (CP: once only) and its entries are applied **in list order, each guarded independently**: an entry is granted only if its program is enabled **and** every capability in its `requires` is held at that moment (an entry earlier in the same list counts). An entry that fails either guard is **skipped**: no event, no receipt, no hash, one line in the ledger's debug log; it is not kept armed.
4. **Consequences:** `--donut-prog` alone at the Juicer grants only `special_short_blink` (Carl's gauntlet is skipped); `--carl-prog` alone grants only `fist_reinforced` and the skipped blink leaves no trace, so `carl_prog_juicer_grants_fist` and every `carl_prog_*` hash cannot move; with both flags the two grants land on the defeat tick in list order, `fist_reinforced` then `special_short_blink`. `--carl-prog` alone never evaluates the safe-room zone (it grants only Donut). The debug flags (`--donut-light-feet`, `--donut-puddle`, `--carl-dynamite`, `--carl-gauntlet`) grant at start and enable no program.
5. **Partial fire:** the `requires` guard skips Donut's entry when Donut's program is on but she lacks `jump_reach_boost` at the Juicer. With all programs enabled, Carl's boss fixture is exactly that case (Carl's pin gives Donut no boost). It is harmless, since the skip leaves no event, receipt or hash. The rule is proven by a stub-scene unit test (DP-A).

CP-A's text states pins per flag and the `grants` list but not rule 3. If CP-A lands without it, DP-A adds it to `src/story_unlock.rs` as a generic per-entry filter (no Donut-specific code) and proves every `carl_prog_*` fixture byte-identical in its PR body.

## 4. Light on Your Feet (`jump_reach_boost`)

### 4.1 What "jump reach" means (DP-Q13)

**Reach is vertical: the jump apex grows by 20 %.** The horizontal air distance grows as a consequence (+8.8 %).

- A ledge's height is what character gates are made of ([asymmetric traversal](../01-design/asymmetric-traversal-and-gates.md): "Donut conquers space ... higher and farther"), and Q10 and DM measure the cat jump by its apex.
- +20 % horizontal distance would need either about +45 % apex (launch about 600 px/s, apex 166 px), which puts her drawn top 242 px above the walking line from a 32 px step (the HUD limit is 228) and opens 10-tile ledges, or a separate air speed, which breaks DM-Q05 (air control is the run speed) and the nav model's `HeroPhysics { jump_speed, run_speed }`. Both are rejected.

### 4.2 Values (all discrete, `f32`, the code's step order, 60 Hz)

### 5.2 Destination search (DP-Q27)

A candidate `x' = x + dir * d` is valid only if **all** hold. The body box is her 24 x 52 collider at `(x', y)`.

1. **World:** `x'` inside `[HERO_HALF_WIDTH, max_x]`.
2. **Collision-safe:** the body box at `x'` overlaps no intact solid.
3. **No wall crossed:** the swept box (the union of the start and end boxes) overlaps no intact solid. A wall between her and `x'` therefore rejects every candidate beyond it, and the search settles in front of the wall.
4. **No protected gate crossed:** the swept box overlaps no **blink barrier**. Barriers are: (a) every intact solid, including a closed boss gate collider; (b) rectangles listed in the room or scenario data under a new optional field `blink_barriers` (absent = none; the generic tag for a protected gap or a HANDOFF gate whose intended solution is not a blink); (c) every story or progression **zone** trigger not yet fired, which the blink may end inside but may not jump across (a 112 px blink would otherwise skip a 40 px zone). Position thresholds (`x >= line` wave wakes, boss engage lines) fire normally on arrival and need no barrier.
5. **Visible:** the body box lies inside the **simulation** camera's view (`ScrollCamera::left()` to `left + view_w`, the same camera as `is_offscreen`) inset by `dpq27_view_inset_px` **24** on each side. The outcome never reads the presentation camera (the Ball of Swine's zoom and the `--cf02` and `--demo` automatic camera are presentation and must not change outcomes). Vertical visibility holds by construction: the blink never changes `y`.
   **Drawn camera (Revision 1, F3).** Under `--cf02`, `--demo` and in the CF02 blink fixtures the player sees the presentation-only automatic camera (`scene_camera.rs`), which eases toward its target at most 5 px per tick, so a sim-valid destination can be drawn outside the view for a few ticks. Two additions keep "visible" true on screen without letting presentation drive an outcome:
   - **Presentation rule (DP-D, read-only):** when Donut is a **framing subject** (human-controlled, or in 2P), the tick that reads `BlinkResolved` sets the operator's `urgent` pace for her until her camera body is inside the drawn view (the existing `something_outside` path; nothing changes when she is already inside). Never for a `bound_only` partner: CA2 forbids her setting `urgent`.
   - **AI rule (DP-Q46, simulation, DP-E):** an AI blink keeps a candidate only if it also lies in the **hull** of her current position and Carl's guaranteed region, `[min(x, carl.x - H), max(x, carl.x + H)]` with `H` = the data knob `dpq46_ai_hull_px` = **63** (108 px, Carl's camera safety region of CA2 3.4, minus her 16 px camera half width, minus 29 px of pan lag: the drawn view trails a target moving at Carl's 3.08 px/tick by up to 3.08 / 0.11 + 0.5, about 28.5 px, and more while the impact pace holds). Reads only sim state. Why it holds on screen: the drawn view is an interval that already contains her body (M8), and the target view contains Carl's safety region (CA2 3.4) while the drawn view only trails it, so the lag margin in `H` keeps the hull inside the drawn view on the blink tick for a destination on Carl's far side (a destination between her and Carl lies between two bodies that are both in view); her new bound keeps her inside the new target view; the pan follower moves monotonically from the current view toward the target view without overshoot (CA2 3.4), and an equal-width interval sliding between two intervals that both contain a point contains it throughout. A blink changes no width (the width is partner-free, M7). So CA2's M8 bound holds with blinks in steady following; `donut_prog_blink_drawn_view` (0 frames outside) is the arbiter. If that test fails during an impact hold, the lever is `dpq46_ai_hull_px`.
   - **Test (DP-D for the human case, DP-E for the AI case):** `donut_prog_blink_drawn_view`, like CA2's M8: her body inside the drawn automatic-camera view after every `BlinkResolved`, at 640 and 960 rest widths, 16:9 and 16:10. AI: on every tick (0 frames outside). Human framing subject: back inside within `dpq46_drawn_view_grace_ticks` **12** ticks of the arrival; the measured worst case goes in the PR body.
6. **Shared camera:** in 2P, `|x' - carl.x|` is at most the separation limit (`max_separation_px(view_w)`, 448 at 640), or no larger than the current separation.
7. **Support:** a downward probe from her feet at `x'` meets the top of an intact solid within `dpq27_support_probe_px` **256**. Only the **destination** needs support: she can blink **across** a pit of up to about 112 px to supported ground, but never **into** a pit or off the world. Airborne blinks keep falling normally after arrival.
8. **Hazards:** the body box overlaps no hero-hurting hazard rectangle the scene declares (the same set CA2 feeds to the nav graph).

The search is a pure function of the tick's state: fixed candidate order, `f32` in the code's order, no RNG, no hash map. Ties cannot happen (one direction, a strict order).

**Gate tagging.** Carl's breakable obstructions are solids (rule 3). Donut's height gates cannot be bypassed: the blink adds no height. Authored gaps that must keep their intended solution get a `blink_barriers` rectangle; the level-data validator of the asymmetric-traversal doc (future) treats `special_short_blink` as a capability that can cross any unbarred gap of up to 112 px plus her jump distance. When that doc's gate metadata exists (gate id, capability tags, solution sets), barriers should be derived from it by default (every declared gate whose solution set lacks `special_short_blink` is a barrier) and the rectangle list kept for untagged gaps; checkpoint zones are not story zones and do not count under rule 4c (follow-up for the level-data PR).

### 5.3 Mana, cooldown and the HUD

- Two charges regenerate in 30 + 80 = 110 ticks, well inside the 540-tick cooldown, so after a quiet stretch the cooldown is the limit; after heavy casting, mana is. That is the intended tension: every missile spent below 2 charges costs her the escape. The companion's `ai_mana_reserve` 2 already equals the blink cost, so an AI Donut that only casts down to its reserve always keeps one blink.
- **Socket (DP-Q33):** CP-B2's `SpecialSlot::Equipped(Chip)` with the Circle glyph and a data label (`BLINK`, 5 characters). Muted while cooling down or below 2 charges. **Cooldown cue:** a 1 px underline in the socket whose width is `ceil(remaining / dpq24_cooldown_ticks * socket_w)`, drawn with the existing HUD palette, and the existing `MANA_FLASH_TICKS` flash on `SpecialReady`. On a `NoMana` or `NoDestination` rejection or fizzle the socket flashes once as the dry press does. Presentation reads state only.
- The mana track shows the 2-segment drop through the existing `ManaSpent` path; no cost preview.

## 7. Final boss: the Ball of Swine tests the whole kit

No ability is added (DP-Q04); the scene pins all three and holds no trigger. When its arena is designed it must give each Donut tool a moment where it is clearly the best answer, and gate damage on none of them:

1. **Agile run and boost jump:** reach a 128 px flank (under a DP-Q45 ceiling) in time; the missile flies level from about 19 px above her feet, so the flank is for repositioning and for targets at that height (detached Tusklings on the flank), not for firing down at the ball; Carl's slower route reaches the same flank (no untouchable perch).
2. **Swat:** interrupt a detached Tuskling's tell aimed at Carl.
3. **Pounce:** finish a stunned or downed Tuskling.
4. **Magic Missile and mana:** keep ranged pressure while the ball recovers, with enough charges kept for a blink.
5. **Puddle Jumper:** escape a committed charge pinned against a wall, or cross the lane to the far flank inside the view.
6. **Survival:** the reflex and positioning, not health scaling.

It stays winnable in solo with an AI partner and in 2P; every outcome is deterministic; the blink's visibility uses the simulation camera even if the presentation camera zooms (5.2 rule 5).

## 8. Gating and goldens

- **No flag, no change.** Every new rule runs only while a Donut capability is held, and Donut capabilities come only from `--donut-prog` pins and triggers, `--donut-light-feet`, `--donut-puddle` or a `donut_prog_*` fixture pin. New events, `BodyDrive::warp_x`, `DonutMana::spend` and the blink state are appended and mix nothing into the state hash while unused.
- **Byte-identical, every PR:** the 20 `EXPECTED_HASHES`, `GOLDEN_WALK_RIGHT_400`, the story hash, the party goldens, the 13 boss fixtures, the `q10_*` goldens, `demo01_*`, the CINE goldens, the whole-route autopilot hash, every `cf02_*`, `companion_*` and Engineer fixture, the ART-INT-02 anim traces, the PLAYTRACE goldens, **and** every `dm_*` (DM) and `carl_prog_*` (CP) fixture that exists on `main` when the PR is cut. The values are those on `main` after DM-A's intended re-pin; DONUT-PROG records nothing before DM-A.
- **Goldens that move on purpose: none.** New behaviour gets new `donut_prog_*` fixtures with committed final hashes, replayed twice, headless and in the render loop.
- **`--donut-prog` alone** on the opening route, DEMO-01 and the CF02 arena reproduces the unflagged hashes (nothing held, no trigger reachable). On the story corridor run it changes the run only from the placeholder zone's tick on.
- **Solo and 2P parity:** the same fixture with Donut driven by P1, by P2 and by the companion, at identical body positions and inputs, produces the same grants, launches, searches, spends and arrivals (DP-Q08, DP-Q31). Only the separation rule (5.2 rule 6) differs in 2P, by design.
