## 1. Problem and target experience

The current demo proves that a deterministic, lit Bevy encounter can be completed; it does **not** yet prove that playing it is consistently fun. The owner's recurring complaints are: basic repetitive fighting, enemy-to-enemy impacts with little perceptible consequence, limited evasive options, Donut contributing too little, weak/undifferentiated visible deaths, and flat encounters. Tone/identity rated 2/5. This is a **game-feel failure**, not evidence that the code for contact, gore or finishers is absent.

**North-star test:** repeat the *same 40–60 second fight* three times voluntarily because the player can invent a different satisfying sequence each time, **and because the goblins do not behave the same way twice**. The fight should be exciting with only Carl and a handful of ordinary goblins, then become more interesting with Donut and with the terrain; killing an enemy by hitting it repeatedly must remain viable. Depth comes from systems that combine (reactions, impacts, verticality, acting), not from more HP, more enemies or longer button strings.

### What the code says about the complaints (main at 46210b1)

These are reading results, not runtime claims; CF02-00 confirms them with traces.

- **"Deaths are always the same."** The goblin species profile (`assets/data/death/species_death_profiles.json`) has one blunt recipe, `knockback_down`, for routine **and** heavy melee. Every jab, haymaker and lethal kick kill uses the same finish and the same lethal knock (160 / 210 px/s).
- **"The enemy falls but you can't use the advantage."** A kicked goblin lies down for only `DOWNED_TICKS` 18 before its 22-tick getup. Carl's kick total is 36 ticks plus 8 hit-stop ticks, and the goblin lands about 90 px away, so Carl usually arrives during the getup. A lying goblin keeps its standing 24 x 38 hurtbox, so a chest-high jab "hits" empty air above the body and stands it up (`Hurt`); a non-lethal pounce does the same.
- **Goblin-on-goblin 1/5.** CP-02 fires only for a **living** kicked goblin. A kick that kills (the common jab-then-kick case) launches a dead body that passes through other goblins. A living contact gives the second goblin a 24-tick stagger and a 1.5 px push, a small result next to the flier. A launched goblin that meets a wall "stops dead" with no event.
- **"Fights feel the same every time."** Goblins have one behaviour: approach to the trigger or stand-off distance, take one of two attack tokens, tell, swing. Nothing reacts to a friend's death, a crash or a hurt hero, and the fixture `seed` is not drawn from, so a replayed fight is identical move for move.
- **"More ways to dodge."** A grounded move locks Carl's feet and strips Jump until its recovery ends (`combat_scene.rs`, the `committed` gate), so a player who swings at the wrong moment cannot get out. Airborne presses start the ground jab or kick; there is no aerial-specific move. A buffered press that fires later keeps the old facing (only an unbuffered press turns Carl).
- **Llama evasion.** The glob flies straight at 42..48 px above the llama's feet. Carl's only answers are a jump or distance; Donut's 24 px hurtbox is below the line. Donut's moves do not hit llamas (WORK-023-F4).
- **AI Donut 2/5.** The follower never attacks (party Q9 B). PR #123 gave the *controlled* Donut her swat, pounce and mana; the AI still starts no move.

### Authority and influences

- [Vision](vision.md) and [combat-first encounter direction](combat-first-encounter-design.md): basic takedowns must feel good; physical and environmental causality; player-authored mayhem; short recovery, not sponge enemies or puzzle-first rooms.
- [Combat feel / hybrid physics](combat-feel-hybrid-physics.md): fixed-step authoritative outcomes; authored, expressive cosmetic reactions; bounded contacts and one-hit identity; no uncontrolled ragdolls.
- [MVP controller contract](mvp-controller-contract.md): South jump, West primary, North physical, East story-unlocked special, R1 interact, L1 solo swap. No mandatory dodge/grab/sprint/twin-stick buttons. Carl never grabs enemies; the contract already names "Carl punches, kicks, **stomps**" and lists "aerial Carl kick/stomp" and a "downward directional attack" as playtest candidates, and asks to "avoid unexplained auto-switches of move type at borderline ranges".
- [Antihero tone](antihero-tone.md): a scrappy, imperfect everyman winning by effort, improvisation and accidents, not flawless superhero martial arts. Original 1980s–1990s action atmosphere and vicious situational comedy rather than copyrighted scene/music/dialogue imitation.
- [Character progression horizon](character-progression-horizon.md): early Carl punches/kicks and environment; pre-biscuit Donut uses cat-like swat/pounce/mobility; Magic Missile only after the biscuit; earned explosives only when fiction allows; no early-floor Mongo or unapproved late powers.
- Merged work this design builds on and must not duplicate: [Q10 Donut moveset](../02-art/donut-moveset-q10.md) with its adopted defaults Q10-A01..A10 and [mana v0](donut-mana-v0-proposal.md) (#123); [CINE-01](../03-tech/cinematic-finishers-cine-01.md) (#122); DEATH-D finishes (#108); AUDIENCE-01 viewer counters (#118); THE JUICER boss (#120).
- Our reference vocabulary: **Broforce** for urgency, readable destruction and emergent surprises; **Dead Cells** for quick response, buffering and distinctive hit feedback; **Mewgenics** for character expressiveness and reusable authored animation; **Noita** for meaningful material interaction **without** reproducing its per-pixel simulation. These are *principles to evaluate*, not an instruction to copy assets, exact moves or rules.

### Non-negotiable boundaries

1. No extra required face-button verb and no redesign of the approved controller layout; context/direction/state may vary an existing move only when communicated clearly. Remapping/accessibility retained.
2. No early Magic Missile, dynamite, Puddle Jumper, shoulder mode or other story-locked skills; probe flags may explicitly grant post-unlock capabilities for tests only and must not leak into the story route. The DEMO-01 hall stays locked (no biscuit, `DonutSetup::NO` until the Guild room exists).
3. New combo depth = **branching consequences**, not memorized ten-hit strings or higher enemy HP. No arbitrary melee decapitations, blast kills, or slow-motion rewards from ordinary hits. Carl never grabs.
4. Preserve the visual 1x/Deck/1080p readability and approved Carl/Donut sprite identities, silhouettes, foot pivots and established industrial catacomb art. Never use concept PNGs as production sprites. New poses stay within the [pose budget](#cf02-i--character-and-enemy-combat-acting-p1) and follow the art pipeline (top-tier design, medium-tier execution, separate top-tier review).
5. Same combat opportunity and solvable encounter in solo and local 2P. The solo AI must not steal the player's wins; P2 must not wait as a spectator.
6. Actor/terrain collision, damage, attribution, cooldowns, kill causes **and enemy reactions** stay deterministic at 60 Hz. Variety comes only from a seeded, stateless function of the fixture seed, the attempt index and the enemy index (CF02-Q58): no wall clock, no unseeded RNG, no presentation randomness feeding back. VFX, screen shake, camera zoom, audio, gore preset, acting overlays and cinematic slow motion never write back to gameplay or its replay hash.
7. **Every CF02 rule is off outside the opt-in `--cf02` scenario** until the owner accepts it: gameplay rules, the Q18 llama targets (CF02-Q37), the defeat-profile overlay, the gore budget and the cue overlay (CF02-Q36). `--demo`, the story route and every existing golden stay bit-identical **and look and sound like DEMO-01**, so the owner can A/B the tested hall against the new room on one build.
8. The live-viewer HUD (AUDIENCE-01, merged #118 as preview and QA only) **stays absent at the start of Floor 01** and is outside this iteration. CINE-01 eligibility is unchanged (CF02-Q25, Q41): slow motion stays rare and skill-gated.

### CF02-A — Short, branching Carl combos and meaningful downed targets [P0]

Keep the quick jab, chained haymaker and push kick. Add three short branches, each with a different consequence:

| Branch | Input | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-the-spot kill | Jab -> haymaker (legacy, connect not required) | Routine kill where he stands (`knockback_down`) |
| Follow-through kick | Jab **that connected**, then Physical (neutral or forward) in the jab's chain window (Q05, Q35) | A faster kick; on a 1 HP goblin a heavy kill whose body flies far (Q06) and can bowl over another goblin (Q10) |
| Ground finish | Kick a fresh goblin, it lies down longer (Q04), then **Down + Physical** stomp (Q01, Q02) | A stomp kill in place; or the kicked goblin slams a wall (Q11) or knocks a second goblin down (Q09) |

- **Stomp binding (Q01).** Variant A, **Down + Physical**, grounded only. Rejected: B, Physical turning into a stomp near a lying target, because the contract forbids "unexplained auto-switches of move type at borderline ranges", it would steal relaunch kicks, and a missed context read is not learnable. Down + Physical always stomps on the ground (or dives in the air, Q52), even with no target, so it is predictable. Physical without Down is always the kick. Down + Physical beats the follow-through: it is buffered past the jab (Q35).
- **Lying targets (Q03, Q04, Q34).** In `--cf02` a lying goblin has a low hurtbox, so the chest-high jab and haymaker pass over the body (they no longer stand it up), while the kick (relaunch), the stomp, the dive, the air kick and Donut's pounce reach it. Every non-kick hit on a lying goblin deals its damage and keeps the goblin down **without extending the current down** (the one exception is Donut's Q38 hold); only the push kick and the follow-through kick relaunch it, which starts a fresh down. The goblin's 2 HP bounds every ground loop.
- **Carl's effort.** The stomp is an ugly, effortful knee-up and stamp with a short lurch (Q02 root step), not a martial-arts axe kick. Its frames come from the CF02-I acting pass (Q73); until then the gameplay PR may use the kick frames as a labelled placeholder.
- **Donut** keeps her merged Q10 moves; her pounce kills a lying 1 HP goblin as before, and a non-lethal pounce now keeps a lying goblin down instead of standing it up (Q34). Donut ignores Down (Q35).
- **Move ids (Q02, Q05).** Appended `MoveId::Stomp` and `MoveId::FollowKick` (H adds `AirKick` and `DiveStomp`). Under `cf02_rules` only, `ForceTier::of_move` maps a lethal `Stomp` or `DiveStomp` to Heavy, `FollowKick` like `PushKick` (lethal: Heavy) and `AirKick` to Routine; the legacy assertion near `combat_scene.rs:4354` stays valid with the switch off.

Guardrails: per-instance hit dedup, bounded stun, no infinite ground loops, no guaranteed instant executions (a stomp deals 1 damage, so a full-HP goblin survives one), no stun-lock of bosses (Q08). Ordinary goblins remain quick to defeat.

### CF02-B — Impacts that create an *observable* second decision [P0]

- **Contact knockdown (Q09, variant B).** A kicked flier moving at least 120 px/s knocks the second goblin **down** (a short hop, then the CF02 lying time) instead of a 24-tick stagger. It still deals 0 damage (Q12). The knocked goblin carries no launch, so it never chains into a third. Below the threshold the legacy stagger applies. A goblin hit in its tell or swing is knocked down the same way: its attack ends and its token returns (Q35). Rejected: variant A (stagger only), which is what the owner rated 1/5.
- **Bodies bowl (Q10).** A goblin killed by a kick is a heavy flier: it knocks a living goblin down by the same rule, logged as a new appended `BodyContact` event (not `GoblinContact`, so CINE-01's "living contact" bonus keeps its meaning). Jab and haymaker kills do not bowl.
- **Wall slam (Q11).** A kicked flier at 120 px/s or more that meets a solid emits an appended `WallSlam` event. A living flier takes 1 damage (a kick-launched goblin therefore dies against the wall, credited `Melee`, force Heavy, to the hero who kicked it); a dead flier only crumples. The layout edge without a solid is not a wall.
- Air relaunches (Q51) are launch instances like any kick, so contacts and wall slams apply to them without a second rule.
- Every contact, bowl and slam is also a **witness event** for nearby goblins (Q60): impacts now change what the survivors do, not only how the victim moves.
- The visual adapter reads the events and stays independent of collision. Cosmetic gibs never become damaging projectiles.

### CF02-C — Donut agency and solo-swap responsiveness, on top of #123 [P0]

#123 is merged: the controlled Donut swats, pounces and (after the biscuit) casts. This item does **not** reimplement or retune any Q10 move or mana value. It adds:

1. **Llamas as hero-hit targets (WORK-023-F4, folded in, Q18, Q37).** Swat, pounce and Magic Missile hit llamas. A swat on a llama tell interrupts it with 0 damage and sets the same light guard as on goblins (Q10-06), so Donut alone cannot lock a llama; the pounce and the bolt deal 1 damage and stop at the llama's body instead of passing through. The slam rule is unchanged (only Carl's lethal kick slams). Behind its own data switch, on only in `--cf02`, so DEMO-01's B4 beat is unchanged. This also gives CF02-E a cooperative answer.
2. **Restrained combat assistant (Q13–Q17, Q38–Q40).** The party Q9 option C, as an opt-in knob, on in `--cf02` only. The **AI Donut** may swat to interrupt a tell aimed at the controlled hero and may pounce a lying or staggered goblin with a pounce that cannot kill (checked again on the hit tick, Q38). Any non-lethal Donut pounce on a lying goblin, by the AI **or a human**, **holds** it: its down timer pauses once (Q38), which gives Carl time to arrive and finish, so 2P has the same set-up tool as solo. She never lands a killing blow as the AI, never acts outside the simulation's scroll-camera view (Q40), never spends mana in the locked demo, and starts at most one move per 60 ticks. The AI **Carl** stays passive (his moves deal damage and would steal kills). In 2P the assistant never runs: Donut is human. A swap still never cancels a move; the AI finishes it and, under the assistant, may start the next one only after the cooldown. Delivered by COMPANION-AI-01 01A as profile `cf02_assist` (CA-21); values unchanged. Under that profile and `ca19_kill_policy = never` (CA-19) the AI never kills, as adopted here; COMPANION-AI-01 01E separately tests `factual` as the candidate default for its own profile set without changing any value on this page.
3. **Solo swap feel (Q19, Q20).** A 12-tick cooldown (from 20) and a camera pan that eases **in and out** instead of a constant 8 px per tick. The pan lives in `corridor.rs` and is authoritative (it feeds llama fair fire and the assistant's view check), so it is a gameplay change with goldens, not a cosmetic one. The 6-tick buffer, the hurt block, 2P ownership and the party-defeat rules are unchanged.
4. **Donut's bail-out (Q55).** The H recovery cancel applies to the controlled Donut too, so both heroes get the same new escape.

Width 1152 and height 660 (the DEMO-01 hall height) leave no void at the widest auto-camera view or at 640x400. Goblins keep a fixed floor, so the goblin lanes are flat; the parapet separates the two waves without needing goblins to jump. The perches are ordinary solid blocks (the engine has no one-way platforms): heroes climb on from either end, leave by walking off, and bump their heads if they jump from underneath. No drop-through, so Down keeps one meaning.

Three solutions to the same room: **direct** (jab/kick/stomp and the air moves through both waves), **environmental** (kick goblins into walls, the parapet and the shore footprint), **cooperation** (Donut interrupts or holds, Carl finishes; in solo through a swap or the assistant). The room also shows the new systems on purpose:

- **G:** five goblins draw five temperaments from a fixed bag (two brawlers, two cowards, one show-off), reshuffled on every restart, so attempt two and three play differently with the same room, HP and enemy count. A routine kill next to a coward makes it hesitate; a stomp or wall slam can send it running toward the shore or into the parapet.
- **H:** the left perch sits over the first wave for a dive; a kicked goblin can be air-kicked into the parapet; the bail-out hop is the answer when a brawler's tell catches Carl in recovery.
- **I:** taunting show-offs, panicking cowards, Carl's effort and frustration and Donut's startle all happen in the first wave without scripting.

- **Falling load hurts heroes (Q30, Q42, owner-requested).** The block's landing hurts any hero in the footprint through the ordinary hurt path: 1 damage, the 18-tick hurt flinch and a knock away from the block, invulnerability respected, the solo guards M1/M4 unchanged. The tell is the existing `settling`/`slipped` states plus a creak and dust from the break; the fall takes 6 + about 31 ticks. Not an instant kill; no off-screen drop (the shore is inside the brawl lane).
- **Authored absurd beat (Q31, Q41).** A goblin swing whose impact overlaps the strike zone of a shore that a hero has already damaged counts as a shore hit. The kill credit goes to the last hero who hit the shore, so the goblin can bring the block down on its friends (or on a careless hero), but the break is logged as an appended `ShoreTripped` event, not as that hero's `ShoreBroken`, so it never feeds CINE-01. An intact shore never breaks from goblin swings. No dialogue, no new monster, no viewer counter.

### CF02-G — Enemy behaviour and encounter dynamics [P0]

**Goal:** the same five goblins produce a different fight on every attempt, because they *react* to what the player does, without more HP or more enemies. One shared morale system (`src/morale.rs`) drives every reaction; there are no per-situation scripts.

- **Nerve (Q57).** Every goblin has a nerve value from 0 to 100. Authoritative events within its witness range (Q60) change it: a friend's routine death lowers it, a spectacular death (stomp, dive, wall slam, shore, blast, decap) lowers it more, a crash or slam nearby lowers it a little, being knocked down lowers it, and seeing a hero get hurt or landing its own hit raises it. Between events nerve drifts back toward the goblin's baseline; drift never starts a reaction.
- **Reactions (Q61).** Only a tick on which events changed nerve can start a reaction, judged on the value after those events: a drop to below 25 starts **panic** (it flees away from the scare for 60..90 ticks, once per attempt, and can be chased and kicked in the back); a drop to below 50 starts **hesitate** (a 20-tick double take; it loses its turn); a rise across 85 makes a **Brawler** **enraged** (for 120 ticks it closes in faster and attacks again sooner; its tell stays 24 ticks, so it is never unfair). A coward idling at its baseline 45 therefore never freezes; it hesitates when something scary happens. The last goblin standing panics more easily (Q62); a cornered one stops running and fights (Q63).
- **Temperaments (Q59), one signature each.** **Brawler:** high nerve, the only temperament that can become enraged (data: `q59_enrage_temperaments: ["brawler"]`). **Coward:** low nerve, hops back out of slow attacks (kick, follow-through, stomp) so the jab becomes the tool to open it up (Q64), panics first. **Show-off:** taunts when a hero is hurt or whiffs nearby, leaving a 36-tick opening that is punished with a long stagger (Q65). The temperament is read from behaviour and the CF02-I poses, never from a label.
- **Group pressure (Q66, Q68).** The two-token attack rule stays. When the second token is free, it goes preferably to a goblin on the **other side** of the hero, so a jump or bail-out past a goblin creates a pincer the player must answer. Reactions return the token, so killing one goblin spectacularly can break the pressure of the others: that is the meaningful decision.
- **Seeded variety (Q58).** The temperament bag is shuffled per attempt by a stateless chained hash of the effective seed (the fixture seed, or a logged `--cf02-seed` override), the attempt index (`restarts`) and the goblin index; the same hash gives each goblin a small nerve offset, its panic length and a short first-approach delay. Replays reproduce exactly; restarts differ; a new session differs only with a different `--cf02-seed`.
- **Not in G:** goblins still cannot climb, jump or leave their floor line; the llama has no morale (Q67); no new enemy type, no voice barks, no new attack. Fear and arrogance need no new damage numbers.

**De-duplication.** B owns *how* bodies move; G only listens to B's events. E owns llama timings; G leaves them alone. D owns *how* a death looks; G only classifies it as routine or spectacular from the authoritative cause and force. I owns *how* a reaction looks; G owns *whether* it happens.

### CF02-H — Aerial combat and movement feel [P0]

**Goal:** vertical play, better buffering and an escape from commitment, with the same four face buttons and no button sequence longer than "direction + one button". Every rule is behind `cf02_rules`.

- **One move buffer (Q46, `src/input_buffer.rs`).** Every Primary and Physical press is recorded with its tick, its horizontal direction and its Down sample, for 8 ticks (legacy 6). Under `cf02_rules` it replaces the `MoveRunner`'s own buffer (the runner gets buffer 0), and a press is offered only on ticks where it can start a move or a listed cancel, so a press made before the jab's chain window still does **not** chain (legacy rule). Ordinary jumps and swaps keep their existing buffers (`prototype.rs` jump buffer 7 with coyote 6, `party.rs` swap buffer 6); this buffer only reads Jump for the bail-out and Q47. Ages freeze during hit-stop, as today. A buffered **fresh** move keeps its direction and **turns Carl when it starts** (today only an unbuffered press turns him); chains and cancels never turn.
- **Same-tick and landing rules (Q47, Q48).** Jump plus an attack on the same grounded tick gives a jump, then the attack's air version on the first airborne tick. A press made in the air just before landing (feet within 8 px of a solid top, falling) waits and fires as the ground move on the landing tick, so late presses do not whiff into the floor.
- **Cancel table (Q49).** Exhaustive: jab -> haymaker (legacy), jab hit -> follow-through kick (Q05), any grounded move's recovery -> bail-out hop (Jump), air kick hit -> dive (Down + Physical), dive bounce -> air kick, and Donut's swat or pounce recovery -> cat hop (Q55). No other cancel exists; nothing cancels hit-stop, hurt or a startup.
- **Air kick (Q50, Q51).** Physical in the air: a short, awkward boot (startup 5, active 6, recovery 10) whose box reaches below Carl's feet, so a jump-in hits a standing goblin's head. 1 damage, stagger on a standing goblin, **relaunch** of an airborne kicked goblin (once per launch instance) so it can still contact others or slam a wall, and the lying rule (Q34) on a lying one. One air kick per airborne period. Primary in the air stays the legacy jab.
- **Dive stomp (Q52, Q53), the aerial extension of the stomp.** Down + Physical in the air: a 6-tick tuck (Carl hangs, visibly gathering himself), then a heavy plunge. On a standing goblin it deals 1 damage and knocks it down; on a lying one it is the Q02 stomp; a kill is the stomped finish. On a hit Carl **bounces** once and may air-kick if he has not already; on a whiff he lands hard (10 ticks). This supersedes Q07 ("no aerial stomp yet") and is **not** a second stomp: same verb, same lying rule, same finish, same audio cue.
- **Bail-out hop (Q54): the goblin-facing evasion the owner asked for.** Jump pressed during the recovery of any grounded Carl move (after its last active tick, not in hit-stop) cancels the recovery into a short, low, scrambling hop away from where Carl faces (or toward the held direction). It leaves a goblin's swing reach about 5 ticks after the press, clears a lying body, and allows no attack until landing. No invulnerability: Carl escapes by moving, not by magic. It uses Jump in a state where Jump does nothing today (the `committed` gate), so no existing behaviour changes and no new verb is added.
- **Platforms and falling enemies.** Goblins cannot stand on platforms (fixed floor line), so the vertical game is: heroes on the perch slabs diving onto the lane, air kicks on goblins that were launched or bounced, and kicked bodies arcing under the slabs into the parapet. A full ledge-aware goblin is out of scope.
- **Guardrails.** At most 2 damage per airborne period (one air kick plus one dive), the bounce does not refresh the dive, relaunches are once per launch instance, the goblin's 2 HP bounds every juggle. The boss and the llama take air moves as ordinary 1-damage hits (Q08 extended).

**De-duplication.** A owns the ground stomp, H the dive; both share `slammed`/stomped, the lying rule and the `CarlStomp` cue. B owns contacts; H only creates launch instances. C owns Donut; H's bail-out rule is applied to her in C2 (Q55).

### CF02-I — Character and enemy combat acting [P1]

**Goal:** Carl, Donut and the goblins *react* to what happens around them, so effort, surprise, frustration, fear, arrogance and dark comedy are visible, using a small, fixed set of reusable acting states rather than an animation explosion.

- **One acting selector (Q69, `src/acting.rs`).** Presentation only. It reads authoritative states and events (moves, hurt, morale reactions, near misses, deaths) and picks a pose overlay by a fixed priority: death > hurt > move > morale reaction > acting overlay > locomotion. Overlays never delay, lock or eat an input: any press ends one on the same tick. No randomness except the presentation RNG for which of two equivalent frames to show.
- **Reusable acting states.** Effort (Carl's stomp, dive, bail-out and heavy landing), flinch (a near miss: an enemy swing or glob that passes within 12 px), frustration (Carl after three whiffs in a row), startle (Donut when something crashes nearby or Carl is hurt), smug (Donut after her hit lands), hesitate, panic and taunt (goblins, driven by G), and the three slammed deaths (D).
- **Pose budget (Q73).** At most **13 new Carl frames**, **4 new Donut frames** and **11 new frames per goblin sheet**, no new llama frames. The budget includes the stomp (previously ART-CARL-STOMP) and the slammed poses (previously ART-GOB-SLAMMED). Anything beyond the budget needs a new design decision.
- **Tone (Q74).** Carl is a tired, ordinary man doing hard physical things badly but stubbornly: knee-ups with a lurch, a dive that is more of a fall, a bail-out that is a scramble, a flinch with hunched shoulders, frustration as a muttered shake of the hand. No superhero poise, no flips, no posing after kills. Goblins are cowardly bullies: they taunt when they feel safe and flee when they do not. Comedy is pantomime and situation (a taunting goblin punished mid-wiggle, a panicking one running into a wall or under the shore). No voice barks or dialogue (AGENTS.md: no invented voices), no obscene gestures by default.
- **Memorable deaths and accidents.** The slammed poses (D), a goblin killed mid-taunt holding its pose for 6 extra presentation ticks before the finish, a panicking goblin flung from behind, and a goblin that trips the shore on its friends (F) give each cause a visible signature.
- **Art route (Q75).** One top-tier acting design pass for all three sheets (key poses, silhouettes, timing and spec), then three medium-tier execution PRs on disjoint source files, then a separate top-tier review per sheet.

**De-duplication.** D owns the death recipes and gore; I only supplies the slammed frames. G owns the reactions; I owns their poses. Audio stays in Q24 (no new reaction cues).

## 7. Adopted defaults (CF02-Q01..CF02-Q75)

All ticks are 60 Hz fixed ticks; tick 0 is the tick the press is accepted. Boxes are pivot-relative (x forward, y up from the feet), half-open. Every knob lives in a new data file `assets/data/gameplay/cf02_combat_v0.json` (loaded, validated and FNV-1a pinned like `companion_magic_v0.json`; CF02-00 lands the **complete** schema with every value below, later PRs only read it), or in `PartyTuning` where the party already keeps its knobs, under the key named after the ID. A master switch `cf02_rules` (default `false`; `true` only in `--cf02`) gates them all, so every legacy golden stays byte-identical and `--demo` looks and sounds unchanged. **Reversibility:** *knob* = change one data value, no code; *switch* = turn a rule off by data; *code* = needs a code change.

## 8. Genuinely open questions

Only these need the owner's hands, ears or taste; every other choice above is defaulted.

1. **Feel of the new verbs and the buffer on a real pad.** Down + Physical accidental activation, the stomp's 9-tick startup, the follow-through window, the air kick and dive, the bail-out, and whether 8 buffer ticks feel forgiving or sloppy. 5. **What felt generic about the tone (rated 2/5).** Hypotheses this iteration tests, with no other content commitment: Carl showed no effort or pain, goblins did not react to a friend's death, nothing funny happened by accident. The owner says which, if any, was the problem.
6. **Where the comedy line sits.** How far goblin taunts and panic may go (pantomime only, crude gestures, or more), and whether a fleeing goblin is funny or annoying after the third attempt.
7. **Evasion and the llama.** Whether the bail-out plus the existing jump answers "más opciones de esquiva" against goblins, or a dedicated evade command (Q26 option C) is wanted after all; and whether Donut's low-risk llama kill (Q44) is a fun cat advantage or a cheese.

## 9. Horizon fit

- **Solo vs 2P:** the assistant answers the horizon's open question about solo AI "without P2 spectatorship": it never runs in 2P, never kills, never acts outside the simulation view.
- **Mongo** is not affected.
