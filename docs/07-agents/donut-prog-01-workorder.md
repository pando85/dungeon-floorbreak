**Goal:** Donut earns two permanent, story-event-driven capabilities on Floor 01 on top of the existing biscuit: Light on Your Feet (a ~20 % higher jump, no new control) at the first safe room, after the first llama and before the Hoarder (~33 %), and Puddle Jumper (a short horizontal blink on Circle/B) after the Juicer. Both use CARL-PROG-01's story-unlock ledger, are checkpoint-safe and data-driven, and are invisible to every existing golden unless enabled. The base kit (DONUT-MOBILITY-01's agile motion, swat, pounce) and the biscuit (Magic Missile, 8-charge mana) are reused unchanged. The 12 s reflex evasion is DONUT-MOBILITY-01's and is not touched.

## Ground rules for every PR

- **Gate:** every new rule runs only while a Donut capability is held. Donut capabilities come only from `--donut-prog` pins and triggers, `--donut-light-feet`, `--donut-puddle` or a `donut_prog_*` fixture pin. With no flag every existing golden is byte-identical (spec section 8 lists them, including every `dm_*` and `carl_prog_*` fixture on `main` when the PR is cut). `--donut-prog` alone on the opening route, DEMO-01 and the CF02 arena reproduces them. **No golden moves on purpose**; a PR that needs one to move stops and reports.
- **Data:** DP-A lands the **complete** schema: Donut's rows, triggers and pins in `assets/data/gameplay/story_unlocks_v0.json` (CP-A's file; DP-A re-pins its FNV hash), and every Light on Your Feet and Puddle Jumper knob in `assets/data/gameplay/donut_prog_v0.json` (names start with the question ID, for example `dpq14_boost_launch_px_s`), loaded by `src/donut_prog.rs`, validated (DP-Q14 range 501..=558, cost ≤ `mana_max`, min range < max range) and FNV-1a pinned. A load error names the field. Later PRs only read values; a PR that must change one says so in its body.
- **IP-neutral contracts:** no engine type, event, field, cause or capability id says "Donut", "biscuit", "Light on Your Feet", "Puddle Jumper" or "Juicer". Ids are `jump_reach_boost` and `special_short_blink`; names live in data display fields and the fan-layer presentation.
- **Events and fields:** `BlinkResolved`, `BlinkFizzled`, `BodyDrive::warp_x`, `DonutMana::spend` and the blink state are **appended** and mixed into the hash only when emitted, set or held. `SpecialStarted`, `SpecialRejected` and `SpecialReady` are CP's variants with a `hero` field; whichever of CP-B1 and DP-C lands first adds them, the other reuses them.
- **Determinism:** no RNG anywhere in this order. Fixed candidate order, exact tick compares, `f32` in the code's step order.
- **Fixtures:** new ones live in `src/donut_prog_fixtures.rs` under the `donut_prog_*` namespace, with committed final hashes, replayed twice, headless and in the render loop. DP-A adds the registry to `--list-fixtures` and one loop line to `scripts/ci_floor01_combat.sh`, so later PRs never edit the script. Fixture hashes are recorded after DM-A has merged.
- **CI gate (every PR, on the final head):** `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --locked --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --locked --all-targets`, `python3 scripts/check_docs.py`, `bash scripts/ci_floor01_combat.sh headless`, and the GitHub workflows `documentation`, `game`, `floor01-scene` green.
- **Review and merge:** an independent top-tier review per PR, then a fix check, then squash-merge by the orchestrator under the AGENTS.md delegation (speed mode). Gameplay is recorded as "merged, game-feel review pending". PRs do not edit `docs/04-production/backlog.md`; status goes in the PR body.
- **Model tiers:** all code, tests, evidence and docs: medium tier (Sonnet). Top tier only if a medium-tier attempt fails or a design defect appears (it goes back to the spec, not decided in code). The blink's production art is a later art order (top-tier keys and spec, medium-tier execution, top-tier review).
- **Do not touch:** the reflex evasion paths and `src/agility.rs` beyond reading the motion and the apex helper (DM-B); `src/companion/director.rs`, `autonomy.rs` and the CA2 profiles before DP-E; CP's dynamite and gauntlet paths; `docs/01-design/floor-01-gameplay-progression-v0.md` (its Donut block landed with the design PR, Revision 1) and `docs/01-design/character-progression-horizon.md` (the orchestrator pastes the spec section 11 row; DM-A owns the next horizon edit).
