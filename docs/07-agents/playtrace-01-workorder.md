## Implementation PR sequence (one mergeable subsystem per PR)

### PT-A - Recorder codec and durable journal (P1, start now, new files only)

Owner: `src/playtrace/**`, fixture/test artifacts and the `/playtrace/` entry in `.gitignore` (the recording directory must never be committed); no other shared-file contact. Implement `SessionManifest`, `EpisodeBoundary`, `PartyTransition`, `SessionControlEvent`, `RecorderStatus`, typed and schema-versioned streams, a stable exact normalized action codec (including analog, no synthetic button-repeat edges), an append-only chunk writer/reader (`manifest.json` atomic rename, `control.jsonl`, 600-tick `ticks-NNNNNN.jsonl` chunks with a trailing FNV-1a-64 checksum line, PT-Q15), 60-tick hashes, crash recovery and the participant/privacy fields of PT-Q16. **No compression and no new crate** (`session_id` from `RandomState` + `SystemTime` + pid). Add strict schema/config mismatch errors; use deterministic codecs for game data, not presentation/GPU output. Document feature flags and the storage path. Provide a deterministic fake party producer to prove two independent input slots, action edges, reset/retry and corruption detection.

Acceptance: unit tests for bit-exact actions, ordering, duplicate/missing tick rejection, same-seed double-run, completed/aborted manifest, unreadable directory, full queue (bound 600 ticks), killed writer/crash, checksum corruption and empty session. Capture-disabled producer gives identical logical game events. No replay schema v1 change.

### PT-B - Live recording adapter, journal-only (P1 after #139 merges; P0 only as the LAB-01 first-human-test gate)

Owner: only `src/bin/floor01_scene.rs` (argument parsing for `--record`, `--no-record`, `--record-dir`; one resource; one pre-step and one post-step call in `fixed_step`; the REC HUD/log line), the module declaration in `src/lib.rs` and `src/playtrace/**` plus the new Rust bin `src/bin/playtrace.rs` (`list`, `inspect`). PT-B may **not** edit `src/corridor.rs`, `src/combat_scene.rs`, `src/combat_scene/companion.rs` or `src/party.rs`, and makes **no simulation changes**. It lands after #139 (no file overlap) and rebases onto whichever of CF02-A/B or COMPANION 01A-wire merges first, re-running the golden inventory.

Behavior: record in `fixed_step` only, after the `frozen` / `is_paused` / `exit_after` early returns, around `step_combat` / `sim.step_party`; capture per-slot inputs (provenance), the routed per-hero `RawInput`, `PartyDrive` changes and the lifecycle control events that exist on main (swap, pad connect/disconnect/Start, D6 pause, `Frozen`, restart, demo respawn, checkpoint advance, exit); a recorder-owned global tick and `episode_t` = `sim.world.tick`; combat events through the recorder's **own cursor** (never `take_new_events()`); state hash before and after the step (`CombatState::state_hash(&SceneSim)` or `SceneSim::state_hash`), event hash (`EventHasher`) and checkpoints every `CHECKPOINT_EVERY`. Default scope per PT-Q12: on for live-input runs, off for scripted, headless, CI, capture and presim paths; `--no-record` is deliberate and visibly reported. New stdout lines use the `PLAYTRACE_` prefix. Storage failures mark the session incomplete and keep gameplay running (strict lab mode may fail early). The control events listed as future in the contract (pause menu, `config_change`, level change, unlocks, companion commands) are **not** invented. Sessions are `observation_is_partial=true` and `training_eligible=false` until PT-C.

Acceptance: launch a live session, manipulate both heroes, swap, pause (D6), die, restart, exit; inspect multiple episodes under one session and validate **every** fixed tick captured once. With recording **on and off**, every golden stays byte-identical (inventory below). A test shows that **CI scripts produce no recording directory**. Include two real-pad tests when hardware is available, with unavailable hardware listed rather than fabricated.

### PT-C - ObservationV2 per hero, effective-input accessor and fresh-process verifier (P1 after AUTOPLAY-02a 02a-B merges)

Owner: `src/playtrace/replay.rs`, the `verify` command in `src/bin/playtrace.rs`, and one passive, **unhashed** accessor `SceneSim::last_effective_inputs() -> [RawInput; 2]` written in `step_live` (the only sim-side edit in the whole program; a test proves every golden is unchanged with it in place). Depends on **02a-B ObservationV2 merged** (open PR #136; 02a-B is asked to factor its builder to take `(&SceneSim, &CombatState, &PartyState)`); coordinate with COMPANION-AI ownership. **Do not** change `autoplay-episode/1` ActionV1/ObservationV1 and do not define a competing observation type. Record `o_t` for seat = carl and seat = donut **before** the live decision, using the sim `ScrollCamera` for visibility and logging the rig view rectangle as a separate diagnostic field; store `o_(t+1)` only at terminal, truncation and segment boundaries. Keep unseen HP/cooldowns/AI internals in a separate privileged diagnostics stream. Verify by replaying the recorded per-hero routed inputs and lifecycle events through `CombatState::step_party` in a fresh process from the reset descriptor, re-deriving the AI inputs; compare them with the live `last_effective_inputs` log. v1 is `snapshot_support=reset_only`; adding `Serialize` to sim types is out of scope for PT-A..PT-E.

Acceptance: unit visibility differential tests, 1P Carl and 1P Donut with AI swapped, 2P both slots, per-hero observation/action pre-tick alignment, content/build mismatch rejection, deterministic world and events, negative replay tests. Required physical devices marked pending until tested. No false claim that offline replay reproduces rendering.

### PT-D - Exporter, human dataset quality and BC smoke (P2, deferred until at least one recorded human session exists; needs PT-C)

Owner: offline `tools/playtrace/**` (Python exporter and BC smoke only; `list`/`inspect`/`verify`/`stats` stay in the Rust bin), without Python in the runtime Cargo build. Build `export-transitions` and `export-bc` in the established CLI style. Preserve sessions/failed attempts, provenance and story/test overrides. Export only verified and eligible human-sourced actions, with per-tick legal `(obs, action, next_obs, terminal)` and optionally named reward/event facts. AI demonstrations are separate, never relabelled human. Sessions with `participants = includes_other_tester` are excluded unless `--export-consented` (PT-Q16). Support actor/hero filters and adjacent trajectory context. Partition datasets by full session/player pseudonym and actual scenario variants. Add schema migration or an explicit unsupported-version failure. Include a tiny real human-driven capture end-to-end smoke; if unavailable use a synthetic fixture **clearly labelled synthetic** until human acceptance.

Acceptance: every dataset row maps to an original source tick/hash/action; no duplicate ticks; legal action, masks and observation timing verified; zero split leakage; corrupted/incomplete/partial observation sessions rejected with reason; successful simple imitation loader/train/validation; actual resulting metrics, not invented policy quality. Keep private diagnostics out of policy inputs. This stage is forward-looking and non-binding for PT-A/B.
