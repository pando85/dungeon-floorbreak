## 0. A coherent city that becomes its own opposite

**One set of architecture, two completely different meanings.** At first the city looks functional: immaculate ivory bird statues, ceremonial red banners, stained amber windows, warm civil lanterns. Below that level, narrow teal sewer lanes, drenched tenements, broken market stalls and abandoned workers tell the truth. In the last minutes the same familiar skyline is threatened by violet-orange wild magic. This requires less *new* art and more **progressive state variants of the same landmark kits**, making the transformation emotionally meaningful and producible.

Visual hierarchy (highest -> lowest): 1. approved playable actor and enemy attack tell, 2. actual interactive prop support and reachable floor, 3. stage route and NPC story milestone, 4. grandeur and danger atmosphere. Lower-priority decoration may not occlude a real upcoming attack.

**Caution on generated city mood illustrations:** a high-resolution original exploration made during this conversation contains a winged white-stone gate, golden windows, distant false-sky trusses and a violet-orange magical storm. It is a **concept illustration** (not a screenshot, native pixel master or confirmed game-design approval), with no imported third-party image reference. It may be added to an importable ZIP with provenance, but not used as sprite art or replace Carl/Donut identities.

## 2. Gameplay-linked enemy art

**Skyfowl guard:** high chest/winged armor silhouette, erect ceremonial spear/banner, obvious civic/non-hostile role. During protected public space, guards do not suddenly animate lethal attack loops; if locally armed in a real fight, show windup and an actual authored attack ID. No modeled wing flight ability without true AI/navigation.

**City elf / 201st:** light angular humanoid, staff and ugly indoctrination badges, distinct from majestic birds. First melee role compresses staff before lunge; second spell role exposes wrist/staff arc before one committed shot. On hit, paper-metal armor breaks in a readable impact; no flags/symbols copied from copyrighted artwork.

**Krasue:** visually distinct from Floor02 floating Mind Horror brain and Vespa: a **human-looking head with attached hanging viscera**; eye and dangling body lift *before* short dive. A native-scale head can be unsettling without rendering entrails as hundreds of red particles. On actual hit, the **whole actor** recoils and drops to a Carl-reachable window, not a floating permanent safe position. Avoid real-person face likeness and unnecessary dismemberment close-ups.

**Shambling Berserker:** heavyweight low movement, ragged posture, shoulder compress then deliberate strong smash, authentic hit body. If bodies can be launched only at very high impulses, preserve feel by local thresholds and an accessible stagger; don't glob more HP onto existing enemy just because it's large.

**Miss Quill:** an elderly official in sharply formal bird attire, **smug administrative authority**, not a battle beast. One neutral talking pose, one outraged gesture and a source-controlled wall-collapse aftermath; ordinary attacks cannot reach her through a safe-region fake boss window because she is not an ordinary combat actor. The final death is narrative consequence, not a repeatable gore animation.

**Featherfall:** visibly mummified/long dead. Presentation-only remains, never a second stage boss or kill target.

**GumGum and Burgundy:** recognizable by a few clean cues, shown as victims/witnesses with dignity. Never create corpse comedy, combat credit or a grotesque collectible.

**Remex:** weak, bound soul leech, almost human-scale against a vast technical/ritual apparatus. Body trembles during the separate countdown but does not have a health meter, combat hitboxes or generic lich spells. The **crystal**, not Remex's body, carries the first bright release; the **rest of the city**, not his hands, carries the final hazard.

**Katia:** source-approved named crawler if available in repo; otherwise only original small protected story silhouette and contextual relay cue. Do not import foreign concept face or add her as third player.

## 3. Six visual state rules

1. The same full-background skyline appears in Chapters D, E and F but receives three separate noninteractive lighting states (calm / broken / imminent). Reduce work and strengthen recognition.
2. The only intense cyan-teal pulse that makes Krasue recoil must have a real damage/visibility/AI cause; scene light artwork cannot silently kill the creature.
3. The office's actual breach must be an explicit source-qualified physics/event transition, never animation-driven collision.
4. The glass case is not a portable inventory item in this game; show it supported by warehouse rigging. The real first contained blast occurs **inside the case**; no sweeping global hero damage generated by brightness.
5. Four precursor pulses have four distinct pictograms/animations / music cues, but **not four new HUD panels**. Preserve red danger-lane readability and normal hero-scale control.
6. Original score/audio themes: public mechanical fanfare -> lamp hum/emptiness -> ordered cult percussion -> sub-bass and one strange failing chord for the crystal -> four *recognizably different* scene pulses -> near silence at final stair. No sourced existing soundtrack or mimicked franchise music.

## 4. Native source production and checks

For each key: (A) full-size original concept, (B) one 1x playable composition with **actual approved hero sprites**, (C) one original editable source kit with tagged static/animation keys, (D) provenance/model and rights manifest plus hashes, (E) independent palette/silhouette review, (F) actual Bevy capture at 640×360 and 640×400 when implemented. A generated 16:9 splash image **does not** satisfy B–F.

**Visual pass/fail:**
- CITY-ART-01: one glance distinguishes official upper city from dangerous lower alleys;
- CITY-ART-02: GumGum is not an attack target, and the city tragedy isn't played for laughs;
- CITY-ART-03: Krasue cues and hit body are legible and distinct from the Horror and Vespa at native size;
- CITY-ART-04: one real physically breached magistrate wall creates before/after spatial readability;
- CITY-ART-05: Remex reads as trapped victim, never generic giant lich;
- CITY-ART-06: first contained blast and later huge wild magic pulse look like **two different** dangers;
- CITY-ART-07: four precursor pulses have distinct gameplay warning responses in actual 1x game footage, no white-out hiding player;
- CITY-ART-08: original full source, manifest, provenance, no copyrighted third-party imagery, owner/independent art review **PENDING**.
