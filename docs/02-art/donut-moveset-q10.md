## 1. Moves

Conventions:

- 60 Hz; tick 0 is the tick the press is accepted.
- Boxes are pivot-relative (x forward, y up from the feet) and half-open.
- Donut's hurtbox is 20×24 (Q4).
- The goblin body is 24×38, and its swing box is `[8, 30) x [0, 30)` ahead of it.

**Swat (Primary, pre-biscuit).** Breaks a tell, buys space, never kills.

- **Timing:** startup 4, active 3 (t4-6, hit tick 4), recovery 7. Total **14**, the same as Carl's jab.
- **Box:** `[13, 24) x [5, 11)`, shin height. It reaches a goblin centre under **36 px**. The goblin's swing reaches Donut from under **40 px**, so the swat never out-ranges what it interrupts.
- **Hit:** damage **0**, push 60 px/s (about 1.5 px), no camera kick. Hit-stop 2 applies to **Donut only**: the target is not frozen, so a Carl jab on the next tick still connects.
- **Interrupt:** a hit on a tell cancels it, returns the token and starts `HURT_COOLDOWN` (20). On a neutral goblin it staggers 10.
- **Glance:** no stagger, no hit-stop, no state change; a 1-tick flash and a `Glance` event. A swat glances on a goblin that is:
  - in its swing (smear or impact) or its recovery, which avoids skipping the 40-tick `ATTACK_COOLDOWN`;
  - already staggered;
  - launched, downed or airborne, so it never shortens Carl's knockdown;
  - **guarded** (below).
- **Light guard (anti-lock, state-based):** a swat stagger sets the guard on the goblin. The guard clears once at least 60 ticks have passed **and** the goblin's next swing has reached Impact. It also clears when the goblin is launched, downed or defeated. A guarded goblin cannot be swat-interrupted, but Carl's hits and the pounce still interrupt it. Result: a goblin facing only Donut lands every second attack, however its re-tell is delayed (walk-in, token, `TELL_SPACING`, retarget). In 2P, Carl's damage ends the lock in a kill.
- **Boss** (#115, consistent with its `poise_damage`): the swat always glances (0 HP, no interrupt, no hit-stop). In neutral only, it adds 1 poise.
- **Movement:**
  - grounded only;
  - `vx` capped at 60 px/s rather than zeroed (the ANIM-DONUT-02 gate decision);
  - facing fixed, jump off on t0-6;
  - a jump on t7-13 cancels the recovery (agility; Carl cannot);
  - a second press on t4-13 swats again at t10.

**Pounce (Physical, before and after the biscuit).** Relocates her about 40 px and finishes weakened goblins. Its risk is the whiff.

- **Timing:**
  - crouch t0-9 (tell);
  - launch on t10 at vx 180 and vy 130 px/s;
  - 14 integration steps (semi-implicit, gravity 1050), about 42 px of travel, 7 px apex;
  - **touchdown** on the first tick that starts grounded, t24 on flat floor;
  - active from launch to touchdown + 1 (pin t24-25), then recovery 18 (t26-43);
  - total **44**, the art's nominal value.
- **Box:** `[11, 25) x [0, 17)`, the flight and pin paws. It reaches a goblin centre under 37 px, so it connects when launched from about 80 px or closer.
- **Hit:** damage **1**, hit-stop 4, stagger 14, no push.
  - One target per instance: the first overlap, lowest index first.
  - On a hit, `vx` goes to 0 and she drops into the pin at its feet.
  - A kill is `DefeatCause::Melee` credited to hero index 1 (non-implied instigator, so only that event's hash grows), force Routine.
- **Bounds:**
  - grounded start only, no jump cancel, and the move never refunds or spends a jump;
  - walls and body contact (centre distance 24 px with a living goblin) stop it, so she never passes through or over a goblin;
  - the 7 px apex mounts nothing a jump cannot, and the boss rack ledge (104 px) stays a cat-jump gate.
- **Lapse (air cap 20):** if she is still airborne 20 ticks after launch (off a ledge), the box turns off and the input lock ends. Air control is then that of a walk-off fall (`jump_fall`), and she lands with `land`, no pin.
- **Commitment:** input and facing stay locked until touchdown + 8. After that, a move or a jump cancels the rest. A whiff exposes her for 32 ticks, longer than a goblin's 26 ticks from tell to impact.

**Shared rules:**

- Any hit on Donut cancels either move (no armour).
- A press made while airborne or busy is buffered for 6 ticks (Carl's value), then dropped; a swap clears the buffer.
- **Neither move is a shore or breakable strike** (cat-scale contact; CP-03 stays Carl's beat).
- There is no friendly fire on Carl.

## 2. Beside Carl, 2P and solo swap

- **Hit heights never overlap:** Carl's jab and haymaker hit at 27-38 px and his kick at 18-28. Donut's swat hits at 5-11 and her pounce at 0-17, so the flash position shows who hit.
- **Roles:** Carl deals damage and launches; Donut interrupts and finishes. Typical play: a goblin tells at Carl, Donut swats, then Carl's jab and haymaker kill (no freeze blocks the jab). Or Carl jabs and Donut's pounce finishes (a swat on the jabbed, staggered goblin would glance).
- **Solo as Donut:** works (two pounces per goblin) but is slow on purpose; swap to Carl.
- **Same tick:** the order is Carl, Donut, goblins. This is deterministic.
- **Swap (Q8):** a swap never cancels a move. The AI finishes the move and starts none. **The follower never attacks** (Q9 B).

## 3. Primary mapping and the 0-mana rule

| Donut state | Primary (Square/X) | Physical (Triangle/Y) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-biscuit | Swat | Pounce |
| Post-biscuit, mana ≥ 1 | Magic Missile (1 charge, 1 damage, interrupts like any hit) | Pounce |
| Post-biscuit, mana 0 | **Dry press (final)** | Pounce |

**Decision: dry press.** The mana v0 interim rule becomes final: no cast, no swat, no lock and no buffer. The press emits `CastRejected{Dry}`, mutes the `MISSILE` chip and plays an empty click. Added on top is a presentation-only **dry cue**: 8 ticks of the ANIM-DONUT-03 `cast_gather` head on the current locomotion frame, with no bolt. It is never hashed and never blocks input. Why not the swat fallback:

- it would turn the last press of a burst into a committed swat with capped `vx`;
- the muted chip would lie about what the button does;
- the pounce already prevents a soft-lock.

After the biscuit the swat is unreachable; the missile takes over its interrupt role. `empty_press: "swat"` (a playtest variant) is valid only while `donut_moves` is true. With it, the 0-mana chip shows an unmuted `SWAT`.

## 4. HUD chips (§2.4, no layout change)

A player-controlled Donut plate shows `SWAT`/`POUNCE` before the biscuit and `MISSILE`/`POUNCE` after it, with a muted `MISSILE` at 0 mana. Glyphs come from the plate's own pad (Q11). The AI plate shows `COMPANION`.

Chips appear only when `donut_moves` (Q10-01) is on and `melee_live` is true (the scene's real melee gate). `donut_ability_chips` is derived from Q10-01, and a test pins the two equal. `POUNCE` is never muted, and no cooldown or guard state is shown.

## 6. Determinism and tests

**Legacy goldens stay bit-identical**, with Q10-01 on and with it off. This covers the `combat_scene` hash table (including `cp01_interrupt`), the corridor and party goldens, and every fixture final. Three rules keep it so:

- The guard is mixed into the hash only when it is set.
- `Glance` and Donut `Hit` events come only from Donut moves.
- A legacy fixture that presses Donut's Primary or Physical pins `donut_moves: false`.

**Unit tests (Bevy-free):**

- **Swat reach:** it hits only on t4-6 and never defeats. It hits at centre distance 35 and misses at 36. The goblin swing hits at 39 and misses at 40.
- **Swat outcomes:** it interrupts a tell; it glances on a swing, a recovery, a staggered goblin or a downed goblin (the down timer is unchanged).
- **Swat freeze:** after a swat on tick T, a Carl jab active on T+1..T+2 hits.
- **Guard:** a second swat glances when the re-tell is delayed by a walk-in from 34 px and by `TELL_SPACING`. The guard clears only after both 60 ticks and the next Impact. A jab still interrupts a guarded goblin.
- **Boss:** a swat adds poise in neutral only and never interrupts.
- **Shore:** a swat or pounce over the CP-03 strike zone never breaks it.
- **Swat movement:** the second swat fires at t10. The jump cancel works from t7 and not on t6. `vx` stays at or below 60.
- **Pounce flight:** launch t10, touchdown t24 (the first grounded tick), travel 42 ± 1 px, apex at most 7 px. `vx` is 0 after a hit. One target per instance. A kill is credited to index 1.
- **Pounce bounds:** it stops at walls and at 24 px contact. A lapse after 20 air ticks drops the box, ends the lock, lands with `land` and plays no pin. It never starts airborne and has no jump cancel.
- **Shared:** a hurt cancels both moves. The buffer drops the press on tick 7. A swap clears the buffer; the AI finishes a running move and starts none.
- **Mapping and HUD:** Primary swats before the biscuit and casts after it. At 0 mana it is a dry press with no lock and no buffer. Physical is always the pounce. The chips follow section 4. `"swat"` is rejected while Q10-01 is off.

## 8. Art acceptance targets (review of the integration evidence)

1. At 1× on lit Floor 1, the swat paw meets the goblin's shins on the hit tick, with the flash clearly below Carl's jab flash.
2. The 10-tick crouch reads as a tell. The flight shows paws forward, with no float and no ledge climb. The pin lands at the goblin's feet, and a whiffed pin reads as exposed.
3. A glance reads weaker than a hit: a 1-tick flash and no flinch. The goblin keeps its pose.
4. A running swat shows no foot-skate beyond what the s2 hind-paw shuffle hides.
5. No Donut pixel is new or edited. She keeps her identity, cute in neutral and never a brawler. Carl is untouched.
6. In 2P at the midpoint camera, both heroes' attacks read at once, and P2's plate shows `SWAT`/`POUNCE` in P2's glyphs.
7. The dry cue reads as "tried, nothing happened", never as a cast. The `--debug-combat` boxes match the diagram.

## 9. Open questions (playtest; defaults stand)

1. Is a 0-damage swat enough for P2 to feel useful? Fallback: Q10-04 = 1.
2. Does the guard read as a rule ("it won't fall for that twice") or as a random failure?
3. Boss #115 owns whether light hits keep counting toward poise.
4. Pounce flanking and the contract's pounce-bounce remain candidates.
5. Donut's faster scamper and higher jump are separate work. The boss rack depends on that jump.

## Horizon fit

Consulted the [character progression horizon](../01-design/character-progression-horizon.md). This design fits it:

- Before the biscuit, Donut has only low-damage feline contact and no magic.
- The pounce stays after the biscuit.
- No new buttons, Carl unchanged, and P2 acts from the first scene.
- Later pounce upgrades are data changes.

The guide needs no update.
