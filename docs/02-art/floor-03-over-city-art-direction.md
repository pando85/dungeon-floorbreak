## Art thesis

**A counterfeit world of infinite depth.** Floor02's cramped pale concrete suddenly opens onto the vast, suspicious night sky of a ruined volcanic city. Backdrops show collapsed stone aqueducts, timber pueblos, theatre trusses hidden at the horizon, a dark slumbering volcano, skyfowl eagle-statue fortifications, and an illuminated red/cream circus. This is an urban action movie made from a predatory TV set, **not** a generic medieval castle fantasy.

The player controls large expressive approved Carl and Donut sprites. Architectural scale comes from parallax, atmospheric silhouettes and in-world revealing compositions; do not zoom playable characters into tiny unreadable figures. First playable frame after S00 should communicate *scale* and a reachable concrete combat lane, not miles of walk-only scenery.

## Visual landmarks and original keys

Each major art brief requires a native gameplay composition proof and one full-resolution original exploration key. Assign *one* unique hero key before generating complete environment kits.

1. **F03-KEY-A0 (Guild):** small incongruously warm internal room, fake starfield visible through a huge arch, three tempting fantasy choice cards, Carl's ordinary posture and Donut's chosen crown; **existing approved sources** for protagonists, not redesigned figures.
2. **F03-KEY-A1 (Over City first reveal):** foreground broken city street with two useful cover pieces, middle-ground collapsed aqueduct, far fake night canopy with support rig, immense volcano.
3. **F03-KEY-B0 (Knife street):** old circus vans, striped awnings, knife-throwing lemur on a *reachable* balcony, collapsing sign / swinging scaffold, readable combat lane. Huge rubber giraffe prop is OPTIONAL original game adaptation; not a statement of novel canon.
4. **F03-KEY-B1 (Signet/Mold):** infected lions in one horizontal play space, a looming planar ink/tattoo shadow under floating ruined banners. Story art must never visually imply Signet joins as a permanent third playable hero.
5. **F03-KEY-C0 (Heather):** a genuine grieving bear with readable parasites, small wet shadow and violent paw windup. Give her character rather than monster-maw exaggeration.
6. **F03-KEY-C1 (Big Top):** eye-level center ring, three broken enormous ribs in **background geometry**, Grimaldi as a massive sentient vine rooted behind physical defending actors. A beautiful yet sad stage, not a silly amorphous tentacle brick.
7. **F03-KEY-C2 (Mongo):** warm healing/guarding room with a growing pink/red feathered raptor and tiny Donut, using the actual approved Mongo growth source.
8. **F03-KEY-D0 (Skyfowl):** high city walls embossed with *original stylized bird insignia*, high-tier guards in imposing silhouette, alleys with normal citizens and warm window lanterns.
9. **F03-KEY-D1 (Krasue):** one floating head and hanging anatomy visible against a transparent teal moonlit background, with clear dive trajectory and short recovery.
10. **F03-KEY-E0 (Education Center):** steel teaching lifts and wings that become contact-driven collapse geometry; movement platforms obvious at 640x360.
11. **F03-KEY-E1 (Magistrate):** a ridiculous administrative desk elevated over cracks in a monumental tower; physical attack can destroy *a particular* structural support without treating entire building art as a solid obstacle.
12. **F03-KEY-F0 (Capacitor):** contained human-scale victim framed by a terrifying technical harness, not a giant ordinary HP boss.
13. **F03-KEY-F1 (Doomsday):** same city silhouette as first reveal, now consumed by a growing white-violet threat, three clear playfield safe lanes, normal hero size and valid stairwell.

**Art reinterpretation of story:** Do not create slavish copies of book covers, existing character art, game screenshots, real corporate marks or other artists' DCC fan art. Do not quote copyrighted dialog. Source renders are concept/reference *only*; sprites must be original editable pixel masters through approved art pipeline.

## Performance-aware art production

**Three layers:** large, inexpensive parallax distant ruins; authored motion platforms/cover props in the playable midplane; readable actors and attack VFX in the foreground. Decorator counts and active light passes bounded; no system to animate hundreds of background citizens as physics actors. The huge volcano, winged guard silhouettes and circus crowd are decorative, not autonomous NPC mobs.

**Scale and source:** approved Carl ~52 px and Donut ~40 px source identity take precedence. Mongo adult approved ~76 px, but size at entrance to Floor03 is tied to his growth/scene canon verification; not always adult. Enemy heights should be set against existing scene hitbox expectations, not guessed from a giant splash illustration. Produce original .ase/.aseprite or the existing text pixel-grid format, tagged animation frames, atlas/export manifest, SHA256 and provenance, first silhouette gate and final independent art review. No single downscale from a large concept illustration counts as genuine pixel art.

**Character acting:** corpses, hit-stop, environmental collision and expressive failure states should be specific to family. The scene's fake-carnival visual busyness must not mask an upcoming tell. Make animation keys for the physical narrative beat (sign impacts, tent rib, bear shove, incoming blast shock) before decorative crowd cycles.
