## Interpretation and source guardrails

- The early on-foot common goblins use **pineapples on wooden sticks** rather than metal maces. Common A and B are visual variants, not an approved brute class. The pot-helmet engineer is not automatically a mine-layer or combat turret.
- A generated label indicating **Scatterers explode on death** is not an approved gameplay feature.
- **Slimes split on hit** is not automatically an approved feature merely because it appears in a caption.
- Rot Stickers are **small, black, rounded wall/ceiling creatures**, not conventional large spiders; use the concept boards for mood, not every illustrative anatomical detail.
- **The Hoarder is not a goblin with a backpack.** Some ensemble imagery draws a goblin-like monster; the source identity as a transformed human requires separate narrative-aware boss production review. This does not downgrade the owner's approval of the image as visual concept direction.
- Printed scale comparisons in the generated boards are not accepted native sprite dimensions.
- No pre-Guild Donut spells, Carl shotgun, or story-premature explosive equipment because a test scene or image suggests it.

The opening art direction (see `../../../02-art/floor-01-opening-catacomb-goblins.md`), first-floor art atlas (see `../../../02-art/floor-01-art-atlas-v1.md`) and combat-first encounter direction (see `../../../01-design/combat-first-encounter-design.md`) remain authoritative for their respective domains.


## docs/02-art/floor-01-art-atlas-v1.md#Canon accuracy and illustrative liberties

### Canon accuracy and illustrative liberties

Evidence was checked in the project's user-supplied Book 1 EPUB (relevant chapters noted above). Do **not** commit novel text, excerpts or copyrighted book images. Avoid treating compositional AI labels, floor plans, equipment and character likenesses as novel facts.

- Rot Stickers are described as **small dark round/ravioli-like creatures** that run across **walls and ceilings** and explode on contact, not large tentacled oozes. Slimes are separate corrosive creatures.
- The Juicer's gym has a **reception desk, turnstile, equipment, mirrors and heavy weights**. Do not infer a specific business logo, copyrighted text signage or unverified gym gameplay.
- The Ball of Swine encounter depends on the **concentric grooves and walls changing elevation**, and its peak artificial light effects are special boss-fight staging. The arena illustration must communicate geometry even with lights switched off.
- Goblin Engineer uses a **cooking pot helmet**; basic goblins later use pineapple-tipped sticks. The opening chase does not occur in the dense later workshop.
- Carl/Donut in any wide generated concept scene are **not** the final characters. Only approved 52/40 px source masters (and approved successors) are authoritative. No shotgun.
- Art-directed contrast **does not imply** a continuous physical route among every illustrated area; topology and area transitions remain gameplay/level-design tasks.
