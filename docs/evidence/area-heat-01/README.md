Evidence for [the AREA-HEAT-01 spec](../../02-art/area-heat-01-spec.md): the kit drawn and wired into room 0 `corral` of `--scene f01_late --f01-run`, the real captures, the offline sheets and the measurements. **The goblin partition is not in any runtime still.** It is drawn and exported, but the dressing does not place it until CORRAL-D (`HEAT-AD1`, `HEAT-OQ3`): drawn geometry must equal the 16 x 48 solid it will carry. Every partition image below is a **gate composite (CORRAL-D pending)**, not a runtime capture.

## Real Bevy captures (`scene_*`)

Every still is the real window of `scripts/room_capture.sh corral corral FIRST:STEP:COUNT` (`--scene f01_late --f01-room corral --room-pilot corral`, the library pilot), under xvfb, in three sizes: `640x360` (`--scale 1`), `1920x1080` (`--scale 3`) and the `640x400` deck view (`--deck --scale 1`). The still is the **last frame** of its sequence: the camera eases per rendered frame, so a still is defined by its whole sequence, not by its tick alone (a single-frame capture at the same tick draws another view). `scripts/make_heat01_evidence.sh` captured each one **twice** (two full passes) and the two PNGs are byte-identical; the window's final `COMBAT_STATE` hash equals the headless run's at the same tick for every line. [`capture_lines.txt`](capture_lines.txt) holds one `HEAT_STILL` line per still and size (23 lines: 6 stills in 3 sizes, the 4 before stills, the bare run): the sequence, the window hash, the headless hash, `hash_match=yes`, `passes_byte_identical=yes` and the file's SHA-256. The drawn view left of each 640 x 360 still (found from landmarks, `scripts/heat01_view.py`; the logged `cam` is not the drawn view) is in [`drawn_views.txt`](drawn_views.txt); the drawn view top is 289.
