# CORRAL-C evidence: the STATIONARY-DONUT gate on the Infernal Corral

Generated by the CORRAL-AUTO evaluator (`corral_eval`, policy version 2) on the CORRAL-C head: horizon 1,800 ticks, 21,384 runs, about 9 s of release run time on 8 worker threads. No Python, GPU or trained policy.

**What ran.** Room 0 on its real geometry (`assets/levels/floor01/f01_corral.room.json`: the pen floor, the 32 px entry shelf, the 80 px dock bumper and the 32 px exit dock), with the CORRAL-B rush and llama party targeting:

- `corral_m1`: the first llama at 848, no rush profile (CRL-Q27);
- `corral_m2`: rush llama B on the pen floor (home 1488, leash 1248..1830) and rush llama A on the dock (home 1968, leash 1920..2016), both dormant until the CRL-Q24 wake.

The sweep covers the named starts M1-A, M1-B and M2-01..M2-13 (M2-06 needs the partition), the M2-G grid, both facings, the attack phases P0..P2 (CRL-Q36), the reflex `off` and `reflex` variants, the parked-Carl decisive seat and the companion seat, every gated static policy (`stationary_cast`, `_burst`, `_aimed`, `stationary_turret`, `stationary_jump_cast` at every offset -3..52 on the named starts and `aimed`), and the mobile, Carl melee and co-op runs. The corridor baselines of CORRAL-EVAL core are the "before" half of the report.

**Not run.** The partition variants `intact` and `broken` and the M2-06 partition-top start (CORRAL-D); the stuck-enemy check (no progress definition for a llama that legitimately holds); live sessions, PLAYTRACE verify and native captures (CORRAL-F).
