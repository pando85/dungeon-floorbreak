## Readings

- **Carl's 80 px jump from the floor onto the walkway is not a real move from the left with the shutter closed** (`carl: 15/16 edges land`). KR2 predicted it (about 8.5 px of apex margin, and the step stands in the way); the validator accepts the edge because 80 px is Carl's class. His real route is the 64 px step, then the 16 px hop onto the walkway, and both edges are witnessed. Every other accepted edge of both rooms lands.
- **Solo Carl, solo Donut (the swap) and Carl with the `cf02_free` partner** clear both rooms at every seed with no defeat (`deaths` is the combat state's own count, which a hero defeat and its checkpoint respawn increment; review F1 of #271 found the earlier restart-based count blind to them), no hero stuck over 300 ticks, at most 256 px of backtrack, and in the court a crossing of the closed shutter over the walkway. Nobody passes under the closed arch.
- **RINGW-F1 (finding, the next PR).** The `cf02_free` partner fails at **near-range** follow across the shutter: while Carl fights g1 about 200 px away on the far side she approaches him directly and waits at the shutter's left face (`companion_cf02_free` court lines: `walkway=[true, false]`). The companion nav plans the crossing (a test asserts it), and when Carl runs on to the exit she does follow over the walkway, so this hurts the feel (Carl fights alone), not progression: the room completes once one hero reaches the exit. The `cf02_assist` partner of the solo runs does not cross during the fight either. The asserting fixture is kept and ignored (`ring_court_companion_crosses_the_walkway`).
- **RING-07:** the reach half is proven physics-exact (boosted Donut lands the 128 px shelf; Carl and base Donut have no edge to it). Its "true owned capabilities" half holds by the story pin (`f01_pin_post_pile_boss` holds Light on Your Feet, K9). No pilot climbs the shelf.
- **KR12's `ring_*_no_dynamite` direct routes:** the pilots run with the full post-Hoarder pin and never press `ability`, which has the same effect as the pin minus dynamite (adopted default, recorded in the PR).
- **Recorded sessions** (PLAYTRACE strict verify, `src/playtrace/tests_f01_late.rs`) use the rig's `cf02_assist` partner; the binary's default `cf02_free` partner is covered by the fixture runs only.
- **The still** is the 640x360 view at scale 2, saved at 1280x720.
- **F6 of the review (a single unexplained exit 101 in one owner-commands run):** on the rebased head, 20 launches of `--f01-room ring_court` (`--exit-after-ticks 30`, xvfb) while a parallel `cargo test` build loaded the machine: 20 of 20 exited 0. `scripts/check_f01_owner_commands.sh` now keeps the full log of any unexpected outcome (under `target/owner_command_logs`, never committed).
