## Approved visual information

Preserve her as an extraordinarily **adorable, round, fluffy, tortoiseshell Persian** with bright yellow-gold eyes, tiny pink nose, compact paws, luxurious face ruff and enormous soft tail. More power and equipment must not turn her into a different cat.

Eight **artistic** snapshots: F1–2 Princess; F3 Star; F4 Strategist; F5 Spellcaster; F6–7 Diva; F8 Summoner; F9 Champion; F10–11 Veteran. This list is **not a verified item-acquisition timeline**.

The source contains tiny mock sprites, a mock turnaround, expression studies and gear icons. All are illustrations, **not** actual fixed-resolution authored pixels or directly usable gameplay assets.

**Do not promote generated captions or small-item icons into canon.** See [Donut character bible](../../../../docs/02-art/donut-character-bible.md) and [equipment evidence tracker](../../../../docs/02-art/donut-gear-timeline.md).
