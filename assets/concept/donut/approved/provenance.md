## Artistic anchors

The approved artwork intentionally preserves Donut's enormous expressive golden eyes, fluffy cheeks, tiny pink muzzle, broad round body and giant plume tail even at the most advanced visual stage. Royal and magical equipment evolves **around** that silhouette rather than transforming it.

Bracelets/anklets are a key design motif, but the illustrator's depicted bands, item names and floor tags remain **research candidates**. Confirm source chapters and item states in [gear audit](../../../../docs/02-art/donut-gear-timeline.md).
