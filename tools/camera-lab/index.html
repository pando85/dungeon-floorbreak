The characters are schematic silhouettes of 52 and 40 world px. Movement, combat and rendering do not replace tests with real sprites, controllers or Bevy. Attacks illustrate causality, not balance or confirmed abilities.

Recommended experiment: enter a room, compare 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× and 1.75×, split the duo, move and jump to see the upper platforms. Check whether you can recognise threats, anticipate attacks and still see architecture behind the whole frame. Repeat at 16:10. The minimap rectangle is the visible area (the minimap is stretched for legibility).
