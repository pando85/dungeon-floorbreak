03 · BODY EXPLOSIONTONAL CANON · UNLOCKABLE
A detonation acquired later splits the body and equipment into fragments. The pieces are cosmetic; the damage resolves only once. No bombs before the unlock.
04 · CRUSHENV-02 · PROTOTYPE
Carl breaks the support and a slab falls on the goblin. Dust, recognizable remains and the detached pineapple club. The block and support are inspired by ENV-02; the base frames are reused and the aftermath is drawn.
Design rule: cause → energy → anatomy/material → outcome → remains. Monsters do not necessarily share the same death: a slime disperses and a skeleton comes apart, while the goblin keeps its ears, shape and pineapple weapon.
References: Carl-H52 melee (48×56), GOB-01 A (64×56), original palettes. Conceptual HTML/Canvas animation; it is not a Bevy capture and does not replace the editable sprites. The deaths are of fantasy creatures and are shown as stylized pixel fragments.
